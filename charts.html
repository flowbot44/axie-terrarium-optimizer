V1.2 — Terrariums Estates

Connected plots of the same environment form an Estate and unlock an extra Atia's Flame boost on the strongest plot.

ConditionRule
Same environmentAll plots in the Estate share one Land Environment
Same accountActivated under the same Terrariums account
AdjacencyDirectly adjacent horizontally or vertically (no diagonals)
Minimum size2 plots
Who gets the boostAuto-applied to the plot with the highest Atia's Flame in the Estate

Flame Power Formula

Individual Axies' Flame = Base Atia's Flame + Evolved Part Boost + Accessory Boost

Your Plot's Flame = ⌊ Total of ALL Working Axies' Flame

  × ( 1 + Land Item % Boost )  ← matching items already include env multiplier

  × ( 1 + Fortune Slips Buff )

  × ( 1 + Estate Boost )  ← V1.2 only, one plot per Estate ⌋

bAXS per tick = ( Your Plot's Flame ÷ Total Flame of all mining plots in your category ) × Total bAXS pool for that category

Land items, Fortune Slips, and (in V1.2) Estate Boost stack multiplicatively on the plot.
